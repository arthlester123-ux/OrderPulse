Automatic data cleaning

  • Trims spaces and normalises column names and status text (e.g. “canceled” → Cancelled, “completed” → Delivered).
  • Rejects rows with missing text, non-numeric or non-positive quantity, invalid price, unreadable date or unknown status.
  • Removes duplicate order_id values (first one wins).
  • Computes line_total = quantity × price and stores dates in ISO format.
  • Shows a summary of how many rows were loaded and why others were skipped.
